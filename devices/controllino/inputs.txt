Controllino MAXI Automation: DRO and proposed limit inputs

Status: DRO telemetry is implemented. Both physical limit switches are absent;
firmware continues to report d6=-1 and d8=-1 until installation/configuration.
No firmware pin mapping or interlock behavior is changed by these read-only
spreadsheet checks.

Proposed mechanical limit-switch wiring:
  Positive limit: dedicated screw-terminal DI0, Arduino A12 (PK4).
  Negative limit: dedicated screw-terminal DI1, Arduino A13 (PK5).
These pins are unused in wiring_controllino.h. This preserves analog inputs and
IN0/IN1 external-interrupt inputs. DI0/DI1 are inputs, not DO0/DO1 outputs.

Use normally closed dry contacts from +24 V to the respective input terminal,
with the controller on the same supply/reference. Expected inactive reading:
HIGH (contact closed). Expected active reading: LOW (contact opened). An open
wire is indistinguishable from an activated switch. This recommendation assumes
mechanical dry contacts; powered electronic sensors need their output type and
voltage checked before choosing wiring. Use industrial screw terminals for 24 V,
not the X1/X2 logic-level headers. Validate both levels and input behavior on the
installed board before enabling firmware reporting.

Arduino 6 is data, through the existing level shifter.

Spreadsheet interface:
  source=controllino identifies the communicating controller. The stepper
  remains a target in device, not a spreadsheet source.
  telemetry_health device=dro:
    Source reception age <= max_age_ms; transport-error field available and empty.
    DRO: reported capability true and effective age (firmware sample age + cached source age) <= max_age_ms.
  reading_state device=dro: finite raw position within inclusive min_mm/max_mm,
    plus only the optional telemetry requirements selected in the row.
    Dashboard zero offset is not
    applied. Bounds may be negative; no firmware freshness flag is substituted
    for the worksheet threshold.
  reading_state timeout_s is required. Optional requirements are independent:
    max_age_ms: source freshness and effective DRO measurement freshness.
    health=ok: available transport-error telemetry with no error.
    Blank either column to omit that requirement; with both blank, only
      finite in-bounds position determines pass/fail, even if the value is old.
    Readiness/capability checks belong to telemetry_health rows targeting device.
  telemetry_health and requested_state retain their existing requirements.
  Blank all pulse/motion operands and reading columns that do not apply.
  Limit switches belong to the controller safety layer; spreadsheets cannot
    select them in device or reading_state rows. Existing controller
    limit telemetry and motion fault handling remain intact.
  set_point=check (or blank): each reading row checks once.
  set_point=on on controllino/dro: passes the initial check, then keeps the
    same position validity/bounds and selected health check active across later
    rows, including motion. max_age_ms only gates the initial row and is not a
    persistent failure condition. Telemetry updates and timer ticks evaluate the
    retained conditions. Disable explicitly
    with a reading_state/controllino/dro row, set_point=off, timeout_s, and blank
    threshold, pulse, and motion columns. Disable rows are checked against the monitor
    before disabling it; failure cannot be bypassed by a disable row. Programs
    with missing/duplicate enable or unmatched disable are rejected at load.
    timeout_s limits the row; it does not expire the persistent check. On monitor
    failure, halt spreadsheet execution and use the existing stop request for an
    in-progress spreadsheet move. Preserve stop failure evidence; no automatic
    solenoid/ESC cleanup. Successful programs disable their monitor explicitly.
  A failed reading immediately halts execution. A once-only row passes and
    advances when all pass. Failure halts spreadsheet execution; these checks
    do not send actuator commands, stop physical motion, or close valves.

The legacy wire fields d6/d8 designate positive/negative limits in the protocol;
they do not prescribe Controllino physical pins 6/8. Physical pin 6 is DRO data.

examples/motion_readings.csv shows the full sequence including an optional
initial ESP32 health row. All thresholds/bounds in it are illustrative operator
choices, not prescribed defaults or validated mechanical travel limits. The
example requires an available DRO; absent limits do not affect its checks.

Experiment command I/O scheduling:
  Device commands execute on a single worker outside the runtime sampling lock.
  Completed command futures are consumed as events on a later runtime poll;
    command acknowledgements never substitute for actual-state confirmation.
  Pending commands do not prevent source sampling, DRO checks, or deadlines.
  On failure, cancel queued dispatch and skip remaining grouped targets that
    have not been sent. Already-sent requests cannot be undone by cancellation.
  Stops use the same queue so a move awaiting acknowledgement is followed by
    its stop, never the reverse. Monitoring and failure reporting continue
    during both requests. Stop transmission can still wait for an in-flight
    command/transport timeout; this is not a firmware safety interlock.
  Retain pending/completed/failed stop evidence and late command outcomes. Do
    not allow load/restart while old command/stop I/O remains in flight.
  Manual motion commands also send without holding the sampling lock.

Move timing and completion:
  Move timeout_s covers dispatch/response and fresh matching started telemetry,
    not motion duration. max_age_ms/health gate dispatch and start confirmation.
  Once the matching command reports moving, remove the start deadline and wait
    for that command's firmware pulse completion plus moving=false. A short move
    may report completed before a moving sample is observed, which also suffices.
  After confirmed start, transient source age or transport errors do not fail
    the move row, and there is no implicit completion timeout. Keep waiting.
  Matching controller aborted/E-STOP/limit-blocked/fault states still fail the
    row. Any explicit persistent reading monitor remains independent and can
    fail the experiment according to its own selected bounds/health requirements.
  Pulse completion establishes commanded travel, not physical DRO arrival.

Unified spreadsheet target column:
  device replaces the former required_devices column. No readiness flag is added.
  telemetry_health: device lists readiness/capability targets; blank checks source
    health/freshness only. Semicolon-separated names must be unique and supported.
  reading_state: device names the sensor whose value is compared. max_age_ms and
    health retain their independent optional meanings; health=ok remains a check
    for available transport-error telemetry with no error. No implicit readiness
    criterion is added to reading rows; put readiness checks in health rows.
  requested_state: device lists actuator targets (compatible solenoids may share
    one row). device=stepper explicitly identifies mode/move targets. Existing
    blank stepper targets are inferred from the move/mode action.
